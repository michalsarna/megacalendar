Print-ready wall calendars, one year per sheet

megacalendar turns a few settings into a large-format PDF calendar ready for the print shop: A0 or A1, month grids or a table with one column per month, your own colours, fonts, logo and background artwork, public holidays for over 150 countries and your own marked days.

  • Print quality. Vector output, every font embedded, a single colour model per document (RGB or CMYK), margins kept within 10 mm.
  • Your look. Separate fonts, sizes and colours for title, year, month names, day names, day numbers and legend; optional borders and spacing.
  • Your days. Weekends, public holidays and private holidays with their own colours and notes, listed in an optional legend.
  • Your account. Each user keeps their own projects and artwork library; the master user manages accounts and project allowances.
